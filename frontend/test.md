- [Download MoonWake to your computer](https://github.com/DXRN-MoonWake/moonwake_kernel_xiaomi_ruby/releases/latest)
- [Download OrangeFox to your computer](https://github.com/mino-ali/OrangeFox_device_xiaomi_ruby/releases/latest)
- [Follow this ADB/Fastboot installation (windows must be full)](https://www.xda-developers.com/install-adb-windows-macos-linux)
- Plug your device in you computer
- Reboot to fastboot `adb reboot fastboot`
- Flash OrangeFox `fastboot flash boot boot.img` (Replace boot.img with where your OrangeFox recovery is located on the computer. For example, `C:\Users\Name\Downloads\boot.img`)
- Reboot to recovery
- Go to menu
- Click flash current OrangeFox
- Swipe to confirm
- Reboot to recovery again
- Go to menu
- Click ADB & Sideload
- Flash MoonWake, `adb sideload moonwake.zip` (Replace moonwake.zip with where your MoonWake kernel is located on the computer. For example, `C:\Users\Name\Downloads\moonwake.zip`)
- After its complete reboot to system
- Enjoy!
